Welcome to **GSBot** — the **G**arment **S**ustainability **Bot**, a
neurosymbolic (Kautz-6) chat + dashboard for fashion and textiles
specialists. It brings materials science, maths and engineering together
with triple-bottom-line ethics (permaculture fairness, environmental
stewardship, real economics), Pareto-optimal trade-off analysis, and
open-standards catalogue/safety feeds to help produce clothing more
sustainably across every axis.

See the link:/gsbot/README.adoc[README] for getting started,
link:/gsbot/ECOSYSTEM.adoc[Ecosystem & Vision] for the design story,
link:/gsbot/STANDARDS.adoc[Standards Map] for the open-standards
inventory, and the link:/gsbot/ROADMAP.adoc[Roadmap] for what's next.
